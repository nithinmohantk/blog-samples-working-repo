# Veltrana 50 mg film-coated tablets (FICTIONAL PRODUCT)

> Synthetic test document. Veltrana is not a real medicine. Do not use for any clinical purpose.

## Section 4.1 Indications
Short-term relief of moderate musculoskeletal pain in adults and adolescents aged 12 years and over.

## Section 4.2 Posology
Adults and adolescents 12 years and over: 50 mg every 8 hours as needed. Maximum 150 mg in 24 hours. Do not use for more than 5 days without medical review.
Children under 12 years: Veltrana is not recommended. No dose is established.
Renal impairment: in moderate renal impairment, maximum 100 mg in 24 hours. Contraindicated in severe renal impairment.

## Section 4.3 Contraindications
Severe renal impairment. Third trimester of pregnancy. Known hypersensitivity to veltrana.

## Section 4.5 Interactions
Concomitant use with Calvorin increases the risk of bleeding. Avoid the combination unless a prescriber has reviewed it.

## Section 6.4 Storage
Store below 25 °C in the original package.
