# Dexapril 10 mg tablets (FICTIONAL PRODUCT)

> Synthetic test document. Dexapril is not a real medicine. Do not use for any clinical purpose.

## Section 4.1 Indications
Hypertension in adults.

## Section 4.2 Posology
Adults: start 5 mg once daily; usual maintenance 10 mg once daily; maximum 20 mg once daily.

## Section 4.3 Contraindications
Pregnancy (all trimesters). History of angioedema.

## Section 4.4 Warnings
Check serum potassium and renal function 1 to 2 weeks after starting treatment.

## Section 4.6 Pregnancy and breastfeeding
Contraindicated in pregnancy. Patients planning pregnancy should be switched to an alternative by their prescriber.

## Section 6.4 Storage
Store below 25 °C. Protect from moisture.
