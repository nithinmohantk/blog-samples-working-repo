# Norisal 2.5 mg/5 ml oral solution (FICTIONAL PRODUCT)

> Synthetic test document. Norisal is not a real medicine. Do not use for any clinical purpose.

## Section 4.1 Indications
Symptomatic relief of seasonal allergic rhinitis in adults and children aged 6 years and over.

## Section 4.2 Posology
Adults and children 12 years and over: 10 ml once daily.
Children 6 to 11 years: 5 ml once daily.
Children under 6 years: not recommended.

## Section 4.7 Effects on ability to drive
May cause drowsiness. Patients should not drive if affected.

## Section 6.3 Shelf life
Use within 28 days of first opening.
