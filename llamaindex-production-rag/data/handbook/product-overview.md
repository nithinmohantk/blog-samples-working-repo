# Larkfield Insight: product overview

> Synthetic test document. Larkfield Analytics and its products are invented for this sample.

Larkfield Insight is a reporting service for mid-sized logistics companies. It collects shipment events from carrier APIs, warehouse systems and spreadsheets, and turns them into delivery-performance dashboards.

## Editions

Larkfield Insight is sold in three editions: Starter, Team and Enterprise. Larkfield Insight Team is the top-selling edition and accounted for 58% of new subscriptions in 2024.

## Key features

- Carrier event ingestion with automatic de-duplication of repeated tracking events.
- On-time delivery dashboards with drill-down by carrier, route and warehouse.
- Anomaly alerts when a route's late-delivery rate rises above its 30-day baseline.
- Scheduled PDF reports, sent by email on a daily or weekly schedule.
- Single sign-on with any OpenID Connect provider (Team and Enterprise only).
- Data residency in the EU (Dublin) or the US (Virginia), chosen when the workspace is created.

## Integrations

Larkfield Insight ships with connectors for six carriers (DHL, UPS, FedEx, DPD, An Post and GLS), two warehouse systems (Manhattan WMS and Blue Yonder) and CSV upload. A REST API and outgoing webhooks are available on the Team and Enterprise editions.
