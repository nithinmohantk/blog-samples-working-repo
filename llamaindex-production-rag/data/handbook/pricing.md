# Larkfield Insight: pricing model

> Synthetic test document.

Larkfield Insight is priced per workspace per month, billed annually, plus a usage charge for shipment events above the included allowance.

| Edition | Price per month | Included shipment events per month | Overage per 1,000 events |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | EUR 290 | 50,000 | EUR 4.00 |
| Team | EUR 1,150 | 400,000 | EUR 2.50 |
| Enterprise | Quoted | Quoted | Quoted |

Monthly billing is available for the Starter edition only, at EUR 340 per month. Non-profit organisations receive a 30% discount on Starter and Team. Prices exclude VAT.
