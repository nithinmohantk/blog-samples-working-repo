# Getting started with Larkfield Insight

> Synthetic test document.

1. Create a workspace and choose its data residency region (EU or US). The region cannot be changed later.
2. Invite users. Starter allows up to 5 users; Team and Enterprise have no user limit.
3. Connect at least one data source: a carrier connector, a warehouse connector or a CSV upload.
4. Wait for the first sync. Carrier connectors back-fill the previous 90 days of events, which usually takes under an hour.
5. Open the On-time delivery dashboard and set the late-delivery threshold for each route (the default is 24 hours past the promised date).

Support for new workspaces is by email during onboarding; Team and Enterprise customers also get a named onboarding engineer for the first 30 days.
